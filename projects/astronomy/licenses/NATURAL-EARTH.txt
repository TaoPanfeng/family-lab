Made with Natural Earth.

The coastline/polygon source used to generate the illustrative Earth texture
is Natural Earth's low-resolution world land data, simplified to 0.2 degrees.
Data are public domain. No political labels or borders are rendered.
The raster coloring and fictional cloud patterns are procedural artwork.
The result is a teaching illustration, not a geographic survey or legal map.

Source and terms, checked 2026-10-16:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/

Bundled source: data/coastlines.geojson
Generator: tools/generate_textures.py
Output: src/assets/earth.jpg
